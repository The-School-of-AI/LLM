    \textbf{Abstract}
\end{center}

\begin{quote}
\noindent Training modern large language models typically requires massive compute budgets, placing state-of-the-art models beyond the reach of most research groups. We investigate whether competitive LLMs can be trained under a severely limited budget by systematically redesigning the training pipeline for efficiency rather than scale. Using these methods, we train a 70B-parameter MoE language model grown from a 1B seed model over four stages and 400B tokens. In this work, we describe our modified model architecture, data mixing techniques on how we build our coreset, and training techniques which have helped us achieve an improved per-token efficiency.Despite the constrained budget, Our model sits at the pareto frontier of performance to training computation, often matching open weight models like while using fewer FLOPs for training and for inference. We release weights, checkpoints, training recipes, and logs to facilitate research on accessible large-scale language modeling.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent Training artifacts are available at \url{https://github.com/The-School-of-AI/LLM}.
\end{quote}
